# RFP-2026-014 — Cloud Contact Centre Modernisation

**Issuing authority:** Northwind Regional Health Authority (NRHA)
**Reference:** RFP-2026-014
**Issue date:** 2 March 2026
**Clarification questions close:** 20 March 2026, 17:00 CET
**Proposal deadline:** 10 April 2026, 12:00 CET
**Contract term:** 4 years, with two optional 12-month extensions

## 1. Background

NRHA operates three contact centres handling approximately 1.8 million patient
interactions per year across appointment booking, prescription renewal, and general
enquiries. The incumbent on-premises platform reaches end of vendor support in
December 2026.

## 2. Scope of services

The successful supplier shall deliver:

- **Lot 1 — Voice platform.** Cloud contact centre replacing the existing PBX, with
  inbound and outbound voice, skills-based routing, and call recording.
- **Lot 2 — Conversational AI.** Self-service voice and chat agents for the three
  highest-volume intents: appointment booking, appointment cancellation, and
  prescription status.
- **Lot 3 — Analytics.** Near real-time dashboards for queue health plus post-call
  transcription and quality scoring.

Suppliers may bid for individual lots. A bid for Lot 2 requires a bid for Lot 1
either directly or through a named subcontractor.

## 3. Mandatory requirements

Failure to meet any mandatory requirement will result in disqualification.

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| M-01 | All personal data must be stored and processed within the EU. |
| M-02 | The platform must achieve 99.95% monthly availability for voice services. |
| M-03 | The supplier must hold a current ISO/IEC 27001 certificate covering the delivering entity. |
| M-04 | Conversational AI must support English, French, and Arabic at launch. |
| M-05 | The solution must integrate with the incumbent EHR via HL7 FHIR R4. |
| M-06 | The supplier must provide a named transition manager for the migration period. |

## 4. Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Technical solution and architecture | 35% |
| Price (see Pricing Schedule) | 30% |
| Security and data protection | 20% |
| Implementation plan and transition risk | 10% |
| Social value and local employment | 5% |

Technical scoring uses a 0–5 scale per sub-criterion. A score of 0 or 1 on any
sub-criterion within "Security and data protection" is a fail.
